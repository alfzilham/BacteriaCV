Summary

BacteriaCVMobile runs entirely on your device. It does not collect your data, does not send anything to a server, and has no account, advertising, or analytics.

What we collect

Nothing.

The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no user identifier. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photo library, or device identifiers.

What stays on your device

When you pick an image to analyse, the app reads that image to run the classification. The image is held in memory only for as long as it takes to produce a result, and it is discarded when the app is closed. The app does not write the analysed image to app storage, to shared storage, or to any other location on your device.

If the operating system asks you for permission to read a specific image, that permission is granted by you, for that image, through the system file picker. This app does not request broad photo library access.

Transmission

None. The app sends no data anywhere.

The model, a frozen ResNet-50 with two classification heads, is bundled inside the app. Inference runs on your device. There is no backend, no API call, and no network request in the inference path.

Third parties

No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no attribution, and no tracking of any kind. The app does not embed any third-party SDK.

Children

The app is a research demonstration tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Your rights and data deletion

There is no data to access, correct, export, or delete, because none is collected and none is stored beyond the single in-memory image described above. Closing the app discards everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this document is updated and the new version is published at the same URL.
